For Developers & Land Buyers

Your Buy Box, Hunted Continuously. Every Candidate, Proven.

You know what you want to build — small multifamily and townhomes, build-to-rent, car wash, self-storage or QSR pads, RV parks and manufactured housing, workforce housing, industrial flex. We keep your criteria running against county data across our wired markets, send you verified Opportunity Summaries as candidates surface, and — when one clears your bar — deliver the full Verified Site Packet: sixteen sections, every fact cited to three witnesses, with a 30-day accuracy guarantee.

“A finder's fee buys an address. Our fee buys the address, the proof, and the file — for roughly half what the pieces cost separately.”
  • 1
    Put your buy box in writing. Acreage, zoning, utilities, access, market — emailed to us so the criteria we hunt are exactly yours. NDA first, always.
  • 2
    We hunt and you see proof, not pins. Verified Opportunity Summaries — county, exact figures, what's verified and what isn't. No address or parcel number until you order: the identity is the product.
  • 3
    Order the Packet on the one worth chasing. Deposit to a neutral escrow attorney — never to us — and the full file delivers in 15–20 business days: ownership and price history, zoning cited to the ordinance, utilities in the provider's own writing, flood at federal grade, permits, title story, and the complete dated evidence archive.

Fixed published pricing — the rate card is the rate card, for everyone. A materially wrong core fact within 30 days is refunded. We sell facts; the judgment, the pursuit, and the upside are entirely yours.

Your Pipeline, Fed and Proven — Without Adding Headcount

Build-to-rent operators, land funds, and investment companies: your own underwriting says it takes eight to ten real candidates to close one deal. We keep that funnel full — your criteria running continuously against county data — and put verified proof behind the candidates your team shortlists, so your analysts underwrite instead of chasing records.
