The Arithmetic Makes This Easy

Bought separately, the contents of one Verified Site Packet cost $13,000 or more from six different vendors over two months — none of it guaranteed, and you'd still have to find the parcel yourself. One Packet: fixed published price, 15–20 business days, every fact cited to three witnesses, wrong-fact refund. Ask for the rate card and we'll send it the same day.

We accept a limited number of research orders each month — verification at this standard doesn't scale like a listing site, and we don't pretend otherwise. Quality over volume is a design decision, not a slogan.
