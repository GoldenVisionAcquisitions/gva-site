About Golden Vision Acquisitions

A Land Research Company Built on Evidence

Golden Vision Acquisitions L.L.C. is a Georgia-based land research company. We hunt land against a developer’s written criteria, verify every material fact three independent ways, and deliver the evidence in one file — so the people who buy and build land can move on proof instead of promises.

We are a family company. Caleb Heyliger is our Manager. Rosemarrie Dorway — a career educator who brought a teacher’s discipline to the deed room — is our Director of Research: she runs the method, checks and rechecks everything, and signs off on every file that leaves this company. We built our research engine from the ground up, county by county, and we want the quality of the work — not the marketing — to earn the next job.

We are not a real estate brokerage, and we are careful about that line. We do not represent buyers or sellers, negotiate contracts, take listings, or take a commission on any transaction. We sell one thing: verified research, at a fixed published price, paid in full before the work begins. What you do with the facts — pursue, pass, negotiate, build — is entirely yours.

We work with developers and land buyers who have a written buy box, and with the professional firms who serve them. If you have an appetite we should be hunting — or a parcel that deserves the full verification treatment — we want to hear from you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight Answers, Before You Even Ask

Is Golden Vision Acquisitions a real estate brokerage?

No — and the distinction is the whole business. We do not represent buyers or sellers, negotiate, list, or take a commission on any transaction. We sell verified research at a fixed published price. The judgment and the deal are entirely yours.

What exactly is a Verified Site Packet?

Sixteen sections on one parcel: identity and ownership with full price history, zoning verified three ways and cited to the adopted ordinance, vacancy, dimensions from the recorded plat, the title story, flood at federal grade, utilities in the provider's own writing, permit history, area intelligence, traffic, and the complete dated evidence archive — every material fact established by three independent witnesses, plus a plain-language section listing anything that could not be verified.

How fast do you deliver?

Fifteen to twenty business days per Packet from the day your fee clears, with a written status update at least every thirty days and day-for-day notice if a county office or data source stalls the clock. Verified Opportunity Summaries arrive on a continuous basis for retainer clients.

Do you visit the property?

No — and we say so in writing on every Packet. Our research is performed remotely from official records and dated aerial and street-level imagery. Conditions only visible by standing on the land are outside our scope, and your own inspection before any commitment is your step. We never promise what we cannot deliver.

Will you tell me whether I should buy it?

No. We sell facts, not opinions — no fit scores, no forecasts, no recommendations. That restraint is deliberate: a research file with an opinion in it is a sales document. Ours is evidence, built to survive your lender's and your attorney's diligence.

What does it cost?

Pricing is fixed and published on our rate card — the card is the card, for everyone, and fees are never contingent on your deal. The fee is paid in full by wire before the research begins — one payment, no deposit and balance, no invoice arriving later. Ask and we'll send the current card the same day.

What if you get something wrong?

Every Packet carries a 30-day accuracy guarantee on its core facts: a materially wrong core fact means a refund of that property's fee. We can offer that because of how the facts are built — three witnesses each, dated and filed.

Where do you work?

Georgia is home base, with ten counties fully wired into our research engine and all 159 mapped for feasibility, plus coastal South Carolina. New counties come online on client demand — if your market isn't wired yet, ask; that's often a one-week answer.
